# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version:

- Be kind. Assume good intent.
- Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks.
- Respect a no.

Breaking this gets you warned, then removed.

## Reporting

Open a [private report](https://github.com/Himato/pointout/security/advisories/new)
or reach a maintainer directly. Reports stay confidential.
